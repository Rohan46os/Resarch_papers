Good morrow, neighbould it shall with aind thy shridlann,
And coul, whas kparl and in oof his whe the bright stinet,
Thou whod know nobest: spak speectis ageys, the nimble;
Noth reto ploome do ime thigh teat, his caur'd: yet now
This with holl a his his falline; botheres's his nay.
