1. Skew-Symmetric Architecture & Invariant Dynamics

In the Antisymmetric-Only Transformer, the pre-softmax attention score matrix is strictly defined as:

S = 20 × (Q R^T - R Q^T) / √7

Because of the antisymmetric commutator structure [Q, R] = QR^T - RQ^T, the matrix is guaranteed by construction to satisfy S^T = -S and S_ii = 0 for all tokens. Consequently, Query (Q) and Key/Reference (R) enter the objective with identical mathematical symmetry up to a transpose-negation sign. This explains why the activation statistics (mean, std, RMS, sparsity) between Q and R remain virtually identical throughout training across all layers and heads!

2. Emergence of Layer 0 Shift Operators

In every random seed (1337, 2024, 42), training consistently causes exactly two heads in Layer 0 to specialize as pure previous-token shift operators:

Mean Attention Distance ≈ 1.001 tokens | Peak Attention Probability ≥ 0.9998 | Normalized Entropy ≤ 0.0007

As revealed by the interactive token trajectory and waveform tools above, these shift heads exhibit sharp localized activation pulses aligned with specific token transitions (e.g. colons, punctuation, spaces), while the remaining two heads in Layer 0 maintain diffuse, long-range receptive fields.

3. Activation Magnitude Doubling During Training

Across all 16 heads, the average RMS activation roughly doubles during training:

Initialization RMS: ∼ 0.224 → Trained RMS (Step 10,000): ∼ 0.408 (1.82× Expansion)

This expansion enables the scaled score 20 × (QR^T - RQ^T) / √7 to reach large positive values (> 12.0) for target tokens, driving the softmax probabilities into sharp categorical focus.
